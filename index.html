About

Salford-based. Detail-obsessed. Straight talking.

I'm an HR Systems Coordinator at Salford City Council, with a background that spans public sector finance, professional kitchens, and a degree in Sports Physiotherapy. The common thread? I need to understand things at the deepest level before I can work with them — whether that's mapping Purkinje fibers to understand how a heart stops, or tracing a four-hour navigation tree to find why an access control fails.

I've completed the GM Elevate leadership programme, built compliance tools to ISO 27001 standards, and I'd rather ask a direct question upfront than deliver something wrong. I document everything I build so you're never dependent on me to maintain it.

How I Work

No jargon. No guesswork. Just clarity.

Everything I create has clear logic, consistent naming, and proper documentation. If I got hit by a bus tomorrow, someone else could pick it up.
